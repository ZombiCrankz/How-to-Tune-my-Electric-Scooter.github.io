/ 03 — NON-NEGOTIABLE

POWER ISN'T THE ONLY LIMIT.

More current or speed can increase heat, stress the battery and controller, and reduce braking margin.

!

BATTERY & CURRENT

Never exceed verified cell, pack, BMS, wiring or controller ratings. A voltage or current label alone does not prove a safe operating limit.

!

FIRMWARE & FLASHING

Unknown firmware can disable protections or brick a controller. Keep a verified stock backup and don't flash files for a different hardware revision.

!

ROAD LEGALITY

Rules differ by country and vehicle approval. A modified scooter may no longer be legal or insured for public roads.

!

TESTING

Inspect tyres, brakes, fasteners and connectors. Test conservatively in a safe private area, never in traffic while troubleshooting.

/ 04 — QUICK REFERENCE

THE SHORT GLOSSARY.

Tap a term to reveal a plain-language explanation.

BMS +

Battery Management System. It monitors and protects the battery pack according to its design and configured limits.

Controller / MCU +

The electronics and firmware that manage motor operation. Exact meanings and settings depend on the scooter platform.

Voltage (V) +

Electrical potential. Battery voltage changes with state of charge and load; nominal and fully charged voltage are not the same.

Current (A) +

Electrical flow. Higher current can mean more heat and stress; safe limits depend on every part of the electrical system.

Firmware backup +

A verified copy of the original firmware and configuration, plus a practical way to restore it if an update fails.
